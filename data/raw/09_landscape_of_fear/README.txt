DATA-SPECIFIC INFORMATION FOR: Woosteretal_Spatio_temp_data.csv

1. Number of variables: 12

2. Number of cases/rows: 5223

3. Variable List: 
Status: Predator status - protected or persecuted by humans
Site: Study site
Station: Camera number at site
Species: Dingo or fox
Treatment: protected, persecuted or dingo absent
Cover: The amount of cover available at each site for foxes
DateTime: date time value
Date: Date of record
Time: Time of record
nTime: Numerical time
Cam_days: number of camera trap days per site
sun_time_minutes: Numerical time relativised to suntime

DATA-SPECIFIC INFORMATION FOR: Woosteretal_Behaviour_summary.csv

1. Number of variables: 8

2. Number of cases/rows: 635

3. Variable List: 

Treatment: protected, persecuted or dingo absent
Site: Study site
Camera_placement: the location of the camera
Cover: The amount of cover available at each site for foxes
Camera:Camera number at site - cameras without fox detections are not included as no behavioural events occurred.
eventID: the behavioural event scored for cautious or confident behaviour
Prop_cau: the proportion of cautious behaviour in each event 
Prop_con: the proportion of confident behaviour each event
